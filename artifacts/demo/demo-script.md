# Demo shot list

1. The allocation problem (10.2s) — 01-overview.png

You have fifty lakh rupees for marketing next month. Where should it go? Mosaic Mix Lab helps you explore that decision.

2. Complete official data (14.2s) — 05-channels.png

We process all ten thousand, nine hundred and fifty official records. Every record passes validation. Email, SMS, and Affiliate have the highest average returns.

3. The proportional baseline (13.4s) — 01-overview.png

The brief suggests allocating in proportion to average returns. This gives expected revenue of about two point six six crore rupees. The exact amount is on screen.

4. The mathematical maximum (15.9s) — 02-linear.png

But the mathematical maximum is different: fifteen lakh to Email, twelve lakh to SMS, and twenty-three lakh to Affiliate. With constant returns, expected revenue reaches about three point three nine crore.

5. Patterns and model limits (13.3s) — 06-patterns.png

Real returns can change. For Meta Ads, returns fall sharply as spend increases. These charts show why historical averages need careful interpretation.

6. Trace every observation (13.1s) — 03-search.png

Every result has evidence. Search by date, filter by channel, or sort by spend. Here, we find Email on January first, twenty twenty-three.

7. An auditable record (14.7s) — 04-evidence.png

Open the row to compare spend, revenue, and the calculated amount. This difference comes from rounding, not an overcharge. The explanation and original values are downloadable.

8. Reproducible and explicit (17.5s) — 07-methodology.png

The methodology explains both strategies and their rounding adjustments. Two independent implementations reproduce the answers. You get a plan you can explore, with evidence you can check. No application has been submitted.
